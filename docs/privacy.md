# NeuroCalm Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

NeuroCalm is a stress and focus companion for students. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is protected.

## Information that stays on your device

Your moods and check-ins, tasks, deadlines, breathing and relaxation sessions, streaks, badges, bookmarks and assistant conversations are stored **only on your device**. The assistant runs on your device, so your conversations are never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see this information.

## Account information

To sign in, NeuroCalm uses Firebase Authentication (a Google service). Depending on how you sign in, it stores:

- **Sign in with Apple:** your name and email address (or Apple's private relay email if you choose to hide yours), and a user ID.
- **Sign in with Google:** your name, email address and a user ID.
- **School code:** an anonymous user ID and the name of your school. No email address is collected.

This information is used only to sign you in. It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used to track you.

## Permissions you control

- **Apple Health (optional):** NeuroCalm can save your breathing sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. It does not read any Health data.
- **Calendar (optional):** NeuroCalm can show your upcoming calendar events in the Planner. Events are read on your device and are not uploaded. If you paste a calendar subscription link (such as a school calendar), the app downloads that calendar directly from its source.
- **Notifications (optional):** reminders are scheduled on your device.

You can turn any of these off at any time in the iOS Settings app.

## No ads, analytics or tracking

NeuroCalm has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

## Your choices

You can sign out at any time in Settings, and **Reset all data** deletes your profile, tasks, deadlines and sessions from your device. Deleting the app removes all of the data stored on your device. To have your sign-in account deleted, contact us at the email below.

## Children and students

NeuroCalm is designed for students. We collect only what is needed to sign in, and we do not knowingly collect any other personal information from children.

## Getting help

NeuroCalm is not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis, call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), text **HOME to 741741**, or call **911** in an emergency.
